How it works

01 · PARSE

Declare

A sentence becomes an IntentSpec in tickers and percents. The language model never names an address. A hallucinated ticker dies at the catalog, not in a transaction.

02 · COMMIT

Hash, don’t instruct

IntentRegistry stores the outcome hash and the policy hash. Only at settlement is the outcome revealed, and a mismatch reverts.

03 · AUCTION

Bind the how

Solvers bid a fee, an ETA, and a vector of guaranteed outputs. Winning means being held to that vector. A bonded coordinator can be slashed for a dominated pick.

04 · SETTLE

Measure, then pay

IntentSettlement sizes every leg from committed weights, calls allowlisted routers, and checks balance deltas on the recipient. The solver never supplies amounts.

Built for xStocks and RWA

One intent can allocate across TSLA, NVDA, AAPL and SPY xStocks with equal weight, a slippage cap, attested-only assets, and a fee ceiling. Rebalances exit and enter in the same transaction, so the portfolio is never caught half-rotated.

TSLAx NVDAx AAPLx SPYx GOOGLx RWA attested

Weights are not negotiable. Floors are doubled. The whole notional gets deployed, or settlement reverts.

Guardrails travel with the intent: spend cap, fee cap, solver reputation, token allowlist, live RWA attestation.

03The coordinator closes auctions so the agent does not have to stay online.
04A solver that wins and walks loses reputation. The next intent can require a floor.
