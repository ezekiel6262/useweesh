Solvers

Bond, declare a lane, compete

The four operators on this testnet are IntentOS reference solvers we run ourselves. They are bonded so auctions are never empty. We are not claiming independent third-party supply yet. A solver that wins and misses its guarantee is slashed onchain, not just scored down.

  1. Post a bond at or above MIN_BOND in SolverRegistry. Drop below the floor and you cannot bid.
  2. Declare capability bits: AI · KYB · RWA · STABLE · AGENT · GASLESS. KYB is owner-attested and checked at selection for requireCompliant intents.
  3. Watch open intents. Bid in the clear, or commit-reveal to hide the quote until the window closes.
  4. Settle what you win. Miss a floor and the fill reverts; reportFailure slashes 10% of the bond to the runner-up.
  5. A coordinator that picks a dominated bid loses auctioneer bond via challengeSelection (30 minute window).
