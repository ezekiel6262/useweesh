Intent Standard v0.2 · X Layer

An intent is a commitment to an outcome.

Not a transaction. Not a route. Users and agents declare what should be true afterwards — a gasless USDG payment, an xStocks basket, an RWA onboard. AI and KYB’d solvers compete on how. Settlement checks the result against the declaration before anything is final.

What is committed

Registry stores two hashes, not contents. Settlement reveals the outcome and policy and reverts if they do not hash-match.

outcomeHash  = keccak256(IntentOS.Outcome.v1, kind, token, amount, recipient, slippage, legs, exits)
policyHash   = keccak256(IntentOS.Policy.v2, notional, validity, fee, reputation, rwa, kyb, gas, allowlist)
intentId     = keccak256(IntentOS.Intent.v1, chainId, registry, owner, hashes, salt, auction, deadline)

What settlement enforces

  • Leg sizes are computed from committed weightBps. The solver never supplies amounts.
  • Each leg must clear max(user minOut, solver guarantee).
  • Output is measured as a balance delta on the recipient, never taken from the router’s return.
  • Spend must meet spendable × (1 − maxSlippageBps) or the tx reverts.
  • Fee cap, spend cap, reputation floor, allowlist, RWA attestation, KYB, and gas sponsorship are checked by PolicyEngine.

Lifecycle

OPEN ──► SELECTED ──► FULFILLED
  │          │
  │          └── failure / dominance challenge ──► OPEN
  ├── cancel ──► CANCELLED
  └── deadline ──► EXPIRED

Bids are public and binding: fee, ETA, plan hash, and a guarantee vector — one per acquisition leg. A coordinator may select; anyone can challenge a dominated pick and cut its bond.

Kinds

SWAP · BASKET · REBALANCE · RWA_ONBOARD · BATCH · PAYMENT

A six-asset xStocks basket is one intent. A rebalance sells and buys in the same settlement. A PAYMENT is a 1:1 stablecoin cash sleeve — including gasless USDG sends — not a disguised swap.

Solver lanes

Solvers advertise capability bits: AI · COMPLIANT · RWA · STABLE · AGENT · GASLESS. The registry owner attests KYB separately. Policy.requireCompliant and Policy.sponsorGas are hashed into the commitment, so a non-KYB or non-gasless winner reverts at settlement.

Agent surface

Agents do not need a UI. POST /api/declare turns a sentence into calldata. The agent still signs. Open questions are not signed on anyone’s behalf.

POST /api/declare
{ "prompt": "put 5,000 USDT into NVDA and AAPL, 60/40",
  "recipient": "0x…" }

→ { intentId, explanation, submit: { to, data } }

Then sign submit.to / submit.data on chain 1952.
Track: GET /api/intent?id=0x…
Record: /intent/?id=0x…

SDK: @intentos/sdk — agent.declare(prompt) then agent.track(intentId).

Trust

Not the solver, not the coordinator for funds, not the mempool, not the language model. You are trusting the router allowlist, the RWA attestors, and the contracts. Encoding is pinned across Solidity and TypeScript by a shared fixture; drift fails a test.
